FAQ

Common questions.

What is "vibe coding" or AI-powered development?

It's a way of building websites with AI tools to speed up development. I lead the whole process and review and fine-tune every detail — AI is the tool, the craftsmanship is mine.

What do I need to send?

Your logo, the services you offer, texts and photos, your location and your brand colors. An "about" section is optional. Don't have everything? No problem — I'll help you put it together.

Can I request changes?

Yes. You'll see the site and can request adjustments until it looks the way you want.

Do I pay before seeing the site?

No. You only pay after you've seen the finished site and approved it.

How do I get a quote?

Just message me on WhatsApp, tell me about your business and what you need, and I'll get back to you with a quote.

Do I need to be tech-savvy?

Not at all. Just tell me about your business — I'll handle the technical side.

Will it work on phones?

Yes. Every site is designed mobile-first and adapts to any screen.
